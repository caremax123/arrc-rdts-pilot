Rare-disease recognition before diagnosis

First exclude an adequately explanatory infectious or chronic disease. Then assess whether a distinct unexplained phenotype remains. The result is a qualitative rare-disease signal, not a diagnostic probability.

ARRC Rare Disease Triage (ARRC-RDTS) is a research prototype for frontline recognition of unexplained phenotypes. It does not diagnose rare disease and does not replace disease-specific clinical assessment. Known infectious or chronic disease must be evaluated for explanatory sufficiency before rare-disease escalation.
